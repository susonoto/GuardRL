# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We aim to make participation a respectful, constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish sensitive logs, credentials, or personal data.
- Discuss technical disagreements in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission is not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the project maintainers through the repository's private reporting mechanism. Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues.

Maintainers should review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
